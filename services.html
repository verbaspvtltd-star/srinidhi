MEDICAL SERVICES

Neurology and General Medicine

Learn about neurological conditions, general medical consultations, diagnostic investigations and hospital facilities. Please contact the hospital to confirm the services currently available.

HOW WE CAN HELP

Medical consultations

Contact the hospital to ask about doctor availability and the appropriate next step for your medical concern.

01 · Neurology

Brain and nervous system conditions

Neurological assessment may be relevant for headaches, seizures, stroke symptoms, dizziness, numbness, tingling and other concerns affecting the nervous system.

Important: Sudden facial drooping, one-sided weakness, difficulty speaking or sudden vision changes require immediate emergency assessment.

02 · Stroke and paralysis

Stroke-related symptoms and weakness

Sudden weakness, loss of movement, facial drooping and speech difficulties may be signs of a stroke. Early emergency assessment is essential.

When to seek help: Do not wait for a routine appointment or WhatsApp reply. Seek emergency medical care immediately.

03 · Epilepsy and seizures

Seizures and changes in awareness

Seizures and repeated episodes of unusual movements or loss of awareness need medical assessment.

When to seek help: Call emergency services if a seizure lasts five minutes or longer, repeats without recovery, or causes breathing difficulty or serious injury.

04 · Headache and dizziness

Headaches, dizziness and balance concerns

Headaches, dizziness and balance problems can have different causes. A medical professional can assess symptoms and advise on appropriate care.

When to seek help: A sudden, severe headache, new weakness, confusion, fainting or difficulty speaking needs urgent assessment.

05 · Spine and nerve problems

Pain, tingling and numbness

Pain, tingling, numbness or weakness affecting the back, neck, arms or legs may need clinical assessment.

When to seek help: New loss of bladder or bowel control, numbness around the groin, or rapidly worsening weakness requires urgent medical attention.

06 · General medicine

General medical consultations

Contact the hospital to ask about consultations for fever, infections, diabetes, blood pressure and other general medical concerns.

Before visiting: Call to check which doctor is available, consultation timings and whether an appointment is needed.

DIAGNOSTIC INVESTIGATIONS

Tests and investigations

The following investigations are referenced in the hospital information you shared. Contact the hospital to confirm current availability, preparation instructions and whether a referral is required.

01 · NCS

Nerve Conduction Study

NCS measures how electrical signals travel through peripheral nerves. A clinician may recommend it when assessing certain nerve-related symptoms.

Availability: Please confirm with the hospital before visiting.

02 · EEG

Electroencephalogram

EEG records electrical activity in the brain and may be used during the assessment of certain seizure and neurological conditions.

Availability: Please confirm whether this test is currently performed at the hospital.

03 · ECG

Electrocardiogram

ECG records the electrical activity of the heart. A clinician may request this investigation as part of a medical assessment.

Availability: Call the hospital to confirm.

04 · Laboratory

Laboratory investigations

Laboratory investigations may help clinicians assess different medical conditions. The specific tests offered, sample collection arrangements and reporting times must be confirmed with the hospital.

Availability: Please ask which tests are currently available.

HOSPITAL FACILITIES

Patient care and facilities

Confirm the availability and operating arrangements for each facility directly with the hospital team.

01 · Patient monitoring

Bedside patient monitoring

Monitoring equipment can be used by trained healthcare staff to observe selected patient vital signs. Ask the hospital about the monitoring facilities currently available.

02 · Oxygen support

Oxygen supply facilities

Contact the hospital to confirm its oxygen supply arrangements and the availability of oxygen support for patients who require it.

03 · Pharmacy

Pharmacy services

Ask the hospital whether an on-site pharmacy is available, its operating hours and whether prescribed medicines can be obtained there.

04 · Inpatient care

Admission and inpatient arrangements

If a doctor recommends admission, ask about bed availability, nursing support, facilities, expected charges and the items you should bring.

Important: Confirm whether the required level of care is available before travelling. Ask specifically about ICU facilities if they are needed.

05 · Emergency enquiries

Urgent medical concerns

Contact the hospital to ask about its emergency arrangements and the services currently available. Do not delay emergency treatment while waiting for a telephone or WhatsApp response.

For a life-threatening emergency in India, call 112 or go to the nearest appropriate emergency department.
