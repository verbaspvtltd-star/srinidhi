FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Questions before your visit

Find useful information about consultations, medical records, investigations and hospital visits. Please contact the hospital to confirm current timings, charges and service availability.

Is the hospital open 24 hours?

Yes, Srinidhi Neuro & General Hospital is open 24 hours. Please call to confirm doctor consultation timings and the availability of specific services.

How do I ask for an appointment?

Call the hospital or use the appointment enquiry form on the Contact page. A WhatsApp enquiry is not a confirmed appointment until the hospital replies.

What should I do in an emergency?

Do not wait for a WhatsApp reply. Seek urgent medical care. For a life-threatening emergency in India, call 112.

What should I bring for a consultation?

Bring previous prescriptions, test reports, scans, discharge summaries and a list of medicines you currently take. Bring photo identification if available.

What are the consultation fees?

The current consultation fee has not been confirmed on this website. Call the hospital before travelling to ask about charges and payment methods.

Can I get admitted to the hospital?

Admission depends on clinical assessment and current availability. Ask the treating doctor or hospital team about arrangements and estimated costs.

What are the visiting hours?

Visiting hours and attendant rules have not been confirmed on this website. Please contact the hospital team.

Does the hospital accept health insurance?

Insurance and cashless treatment arrangements have not been confirmed. Contact the hospital and your insurer before treatment.

Where is Srinidhi Hospital?

The supplied address is opposite Siddhartha Scan Center, Doddipatlavari Thota Street, Venkatarayapuram, Tanuku, Andhra Pradesh 534211.

Are doctor timings fixed?

Doctor schedules can vary. Call the hospital to confirm the doctor, consultation day and time before travelling.

Does the hospital provide neurological consultations?

The hospital website describes neurology services. Call to confirm doctor availability and the appropriate consultation time.

Are EEG, ECG and NCS tests available?

Please contact the hospital to confirm which investigations are currently available, their charges and whether preparation is required.

Do I need to prepare before a medical test?

Preparation depends on the test. Ask the hospital or your treating clinician for instructions before the test. Do not stop prescribed medicines unless your clinician instructs you to do so.

How can I ask about test reports?

Contact the hospital team to confirm report availability, collection timings and any follow-up consultation requirements.
