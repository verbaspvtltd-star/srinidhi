PATIENT CARE

Prepare for your visit

A simple guide to help patients and family members prepare for a consultation, medical investigation or hospital admission.

BEFORE YOU ARRIVE

Your visit checklist

  1. 1. Contact the hospital

    Call before travelling to ask about doctor availability, consultation timings, current consultation fees and whether an appointment is needed.

  2. 2. Bring your medical records

    Carry previous prescriptions, blood test results, scan reports, discharge summaries and details of your current medicines. Bring relevant neurology or other specialist reports if available.

  3. 3. Keep important details ready

    Be prepared to explain your symptoms, when they started, any existing medical conditions, allergies and medicines you take. Bring photo identification if available.

  4. 4. Ask about tests

    If a medical investigation is advised, ask whether you need to fast, stop or adjust any medicine, or bring previous reports. Follow the instructions given by your healthcare professional.

  5. 5. Understand admission arrangements

    If admission is recommended, ask about the proposed care plan, estimated charges, room availability, attendant arrangements and personal items to bring.

IMPORTANT QUESTIONS

What to ask the hospital team

  • Which doctor is available and when?
  • What is the current consultation fee?
  • Are any tests or previous reports needed?
  • What are the payment methods and estimated charges?
  • Are admission and room facilities available?
  • What are the visiting hours and attendant rules?
  • Is insurance or cashless treatment accepted?
  • How can I obtain reports or follow-up instructions?
Please confirm consultation timings, test availability, charges, insurance arrangements and hospital policies directly with the hospital. These details may change.

Need help planning your visit?

Contact the hospital team before travelling if you need information about appointments, consultations or admission arrangements.

In a medical emergency
